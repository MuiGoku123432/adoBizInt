| P-12 | HTTP 5xx on a write maps to `outcome:"unknown"`, exit 5 | A 5xx on a non-idempotent POST/PATCH genuinely leaves the outcome unresolved (04-RESEARCH Risk 6, Q2). |
| P-13 | A transport error with no HTTP status maps to `outcome:"unknown"`, exit 5 | D-09's principle: claiming "nothing happened" is the worse error (04-RESEARCH Q3). |
