<objective>
Retry rate-limited reads a bounded number of times in the service layer, never writes, and tell CLI callers in one stderr line that the run was throttled (PLAT-03, ROADMAP SC3 read half; D-03, D-05, D-06, D-07, D-08).

Purpose: a 429 on a read is usually transient and the pacer already waits as instructed, so two retries recover most cases without hiding a persistent limit; the transport cannot tell a read POST (WIQL) from a write POST (queue build), so retries live at the narrow interfaces, where reads and writes are distinct methods.
Output: internal/ado/retry.go with decorators wired in NewClient, Client.FetchRetries, the stderr summary line.
</objective>
